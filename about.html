About Kimi to Haru

An original slice-of-life anime series and the website that carries it.

The Project

Kimi to Haru (君と春) — "You and Spring" — is an original anime series set in the fictional seaside town of Hoshikawa City. It follows Ren Aizawa and Hina Tachibana through a single school year, told across slice-of-life, coming-of-age, and slow-romance episodes, with a companion manga adaptation and a full original soundtrack.

About This Site

This website serves as the official home for the series: episode guides, character profiles, a webtoon-style manga reader, artwork gallery, and music player, all wrapped in a warm, cinematic design meant to echo the show's own tone.
