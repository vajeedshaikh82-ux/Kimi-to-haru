The Story

A quiet town, a returning spring, and a friendship worth writing songs about.

In the seaside town of Hoshikawa, high schooler Ren Aizawa spends his days between the music room and the rooftop, until a camera-toting transfer student named Hina Tachibana wanders into his quiet routine — and asks him to help her photograph a whole year of small, ordinary miracles.
